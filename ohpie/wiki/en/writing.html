Writing and submission

Structured drafting, figure and statistics checks, simulated review, grants and rebuttals.

The hard part of writing is rarely producing sentences. It is that each sentence has to have something behind it. Every action on this page is organised around that.

Structured drafting

Journal-standard structured drafting works section by section: decide what each section must establish, then which sentences do it, then check the support for each one. Because it is workspace-aware it cites real files under experiments/ and manuscript/ instead of inventing data.

Outputs land in manuscript/manuscript/, with intermediate and final drafts named separately.

Simulated review

Multi-expert independent mock review selects a review process by document type, has each expert respond independently, and requires every comment to point at a sentence or a figure. Its value is seeing "the three questions a reviewer will definitely ask" before submission.

Rebuttal traceability matrix takes real referee comments apart: comment, response, where the text changed, and the evidence for the change — delivered as one table.

Grants

w77-nfsc-pipeline covers NSFC and key R&D programme proposals:

  1. Guideline and funding-trend mining (original policy text plus recent awards)
  2. Multi-source real literature retrieval and prior-work summary
  3. Scientific question and method route
  4. Draw.io technical roadmap and Gantt chart
  5. Simulated blind review in the funding-panel register

Outputs stay in manuscript/manuscript/ and the figures directory, on the same evidence as the main text.

Language

Chinese and English drafts are maintained separately — do not machine-translate one into the other. The language parts of nature-skills handle idiomatic academic English, but they do not decide what you are allowed to claim.

Pre-submission check

  • Every figure is cited in the text, in the same order.
  • Every citation points at the specific place that supports its sentence.
  • Statistics report effect sizes and uncertainty, not only p values.
  • Limitations and unresolved questions are in the body, not buried in supplementary material.

Writing packs do not guarantee acceptance. They guarantee that gaps were written down rather than covered up before submission.
